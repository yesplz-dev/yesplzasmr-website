About the channel

Relaxation, filmed with the same care as the craft itself.

Yes Plz ASMR started in 2023 with a simple idea: point a good microphone at the everyday rituals that already sound incredible — clippers, straight razors, running water, a barber's hands — and let the room do the rest.

Since then it's grown into one of the most-watched ASMR channels built around real service businesses, with viewers who return specifically for the sound of a hot towel unfolding or a proper Tokyo barbershop shave.

For barbershops, salons & spas

Have your business filmed. Let a million people watch you work.

We visit one shop at a time and film a full, unhurried session — a real client, a real service, captured close enough to hear the scissors. No actors, no set. The final video goes out to our audience across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, with your shop credited and linked everywhere it appears.

Most of the barbershops and spas we've filmed see a steady lift in bookings for weeks after a video goes live, plus a library of professional footage they keep for their own marketing.
